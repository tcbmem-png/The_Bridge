Provenance

The only picture that carries the provenance of every record.

Where each number came from — yours, the payer's, or the RCM's — never blurred together, down to the source file and line. A figure you can follow to its origin is a figure you don't have to take on faith. No dashboard you're sold gives you that. This one does.

The floor

The last $70K is judgment, not data.

Write-offs and claims left un-appealed are internal decisions — there's no outside document to check them against. So the engine shows them, and the pattern, and the one that doesn't fit. Whether each was the right call is yours to make, with your people. It can't be proven from the data, and the tool won't pretend otherwise.
